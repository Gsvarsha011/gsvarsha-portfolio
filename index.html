About me

I started with visuals.

Then I looked deeper.

Now, I design experiences.

Hey, I’m Varsha.

I’m someone who gets curious about a lot of things. I studied visual communication, started exploring design, and eventually found myself drawn to the world of digital products.

I like figuring things out, noticing little details, experimenting with ideas and occasionally going down rabbit holes just because something caught my attention.

There’s still a lot I want to learn, make and explore. This little corner of the internet is a collection of some of that.

My toolset

I design in Figma and Framer, explore ideas with Claude, craft visuals in Illustrator and Photoshop, prototype with Cursor and edit in Premiere Pro.
